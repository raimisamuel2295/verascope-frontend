The problem

Images are getting harder to take at face value.

The same picture can move through half a dozen platforms before it reaches you, and generative models keep closing the gap between a photograph and a synthetic image built to look like one.

  • Messaging appsWhatsApp, Telegram
  • Social platformsX, Instagram
  • News sitesWire photos, user submissions
  • Email & forwardsNo original source attached
  • Open webRe-hosted, cropped, recompressed

By the time an image reaches you, it has usually been copied, compressed, and stripped of everything that would tell you where it came from.

Which is the question Verascope was built to answer.
